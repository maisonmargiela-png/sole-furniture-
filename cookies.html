What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device.

What we use

Our website does not set any cookies of its own. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

It does use your browser’s local storage to remember your basket and your light or dark mode choice. This stays on your device, we do not receive it, and it is not used to track you.

If you pay online through our payment provider, [Stripe], it may set cookies that are needed to process your payment and prevent fraud. These are essential, and are covered by Stripe’s own cookie policy.

Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop checkout from working.
