Our story

Sole Furniture started with a simple idea: good furniture should be easy to find, honest about what it is made of, and a pleasure to live with. We are a small team, and we choose every piece ourselves.

Furniture shopping can be confusing, with vague descriptions and hidden details. So every product page lists the size, the materials and how the piece arrives, including when something is engineered wood or metal rather than solid wood.
